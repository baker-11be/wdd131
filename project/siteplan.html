Website Planning Document

Site Name

Trail Compass

This name was chosen because it communicates direction, confidence, and exploration for hikers and outdoor travelers. "Trail" reflects the main subject of the site and "Compass" suggests trusted guidance.

Optional domain availability: trailcompassguide.com

Site Purpose

Trail Compass provides practical hiking information for local and visiting trail users. The site offers trail profiles, difficulty and distance details, weather and gear guidance, and a contact form for users to ask questions or suggest new trails.

Scenarios

  • Which beginner-friendly trail is best for a family with children this weekend?
  • What safety gear should I bring for a high-elevation hike in changing weather?

Color Scheme

  • Forest Green (#2f5d50): main headings, buttons, and navigation accents.
  • Sand (#f4e9da): page background and section cards for a warm natural feel.
  • Slate (#1f2933): body text for high readability and contrast.
